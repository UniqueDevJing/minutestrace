# Brief facts

- Product: AI meeting minutes workspace.
- Users: employees, meeting owners and administrators.
- Core flow: create meeting, upload media, transcribe, map speakers, generate minutes, review with an Agent, approve revisions.
- Differentiator: decisions and action items link back to transcript evidence and timestamps.
- Required operational facts: long tasks persist, can be interrupted, and can resume after restart.
- Product limit: the model proposes revisions; a person approves the only write to formal minutes.
